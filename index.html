Raju Nekadi

I'm an AI Data Engineer at CVS Health on the Voice Analytics team, where I build Call Intelligence, an analytical application that turns customer conversations into actionable insight, and Call360, which surfaces a member's complete history to customer service agents in seconds.

I completed my master's in Computer Science at the University of Missouri–Kansas City. After hours I study Generative AI Agents and Voice and Text AI Agents, and I write about the challenges I face and what I learn. This page collects my projects, posts, and research.
